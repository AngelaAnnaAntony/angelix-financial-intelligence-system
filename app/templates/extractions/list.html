How extraction review works

ANGELIX keeps extracted information separate from trusted financial records until a reviewer verifies and approves it.

1
Extract

Data is extracted from the uploaded document using OCR and document parsing.

2
Detect

ANGELIX identifies the document and financial classification context.

3
Review

Verify dates, descriptions, amounts, sections and classifications.

4
Approve

Approved records become trusted transactions or financial-statement line items.
